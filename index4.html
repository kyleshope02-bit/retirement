Step 4 — How to do a Roth conversion at Fidelity
1
Log in → Accounts & Trade → Transfer → Roth IRA Conversion
Select "Roth IRA Conversion" from the transfer type menu
2
Choose source account — do Dad's and Mom's separately
Each IRA is its own account — convert from each independently, e.g. $22,500 from each = $45,000 total
3
Enter dollar amount (not shares)
Use the amount from the Decisions tab based on your IRMAA headroom for the year
4
Do NOT withhold taxes from the conversion — say No when asked
Pay via IRS estimated payment (IRS.gov/payments) by Jan 15. Withholding from IRA shrinks your Roth and wastes tax-free space.
5
Confirm and submit — funds transfer same day
You'll receive a 1099-R in January. Report on Form 8606 when filing taxes.
6
Invest the converted cash immediately in Roth — buy FZROX
Money lands as cash in the Roth IRA. Don't leave it idle — buy FZROX so it's compounding tax-free.
Step 5 — RMD management at Fidelity (age 73+)

Where: Fidelity.com → Accounts & Trade → Portfolio → RMD Center. Fidelity auto-calculates each January.

✓Dad and Mom's IRAs each have a separate RMD — calculate and withdraw from each independently
✓Take RMD BEFORE any Roth conversion that year — RMD dollars cannot be converted
✓Set up automatic RMD in October — direct to Fidelity Cash Management checking
✗Missing RMD = 25% penalty on missed amount — set Dec 1 calendar reminder as backup every year
!First RMD at 73 can be delayed to April 1 next year — but two hit that year. Usually better to take it in year you turn 73.
