Step 5 — RMD calculator at Fidelity (age 73+)

Where to find it: Fidelity.com → Accounts & Trade → Portfolio → RMD Center. Fidelity automatically calculates the RMD for each IRA account every January.

Important mechanics:

✓Dad and Mom's IRAs each have a separate RMD — calculate and withdraw from each account independently
✓RMD must be taken before any Roth conversion that year — RMD dollars cannot be converted
✓Set up automatic RMD distribution in October — transfer to Fidelity CMA checking account
✗Missing an RMD triggers a 25% penalty on the missed amount — set a Dec 1 calendar reminder as backup
!Year of turning 73: first RMD can be delayed to April 1 of the following year — but then two RMDs hit that year, doubling the income. Usually better to take it in the year you turn 73.
